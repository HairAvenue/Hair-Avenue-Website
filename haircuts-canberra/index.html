A cut that works with your hair, not against it

Every haircut starts with a conversation: how you wear your hair day to day, how much time you give it in the morning, and what you want to change. Then your stylist cuts for your texture — curl-by-curl dry cutting for curls and waves, soft layers for fine hair, weight removal for thick hair — so the shape still looks good when you style it at home, not just when you leave the chair.

Hair Avenue is a hairdresser for everyone: women, men and kids are all welcome, and you’ll be looked after in a relaxed salon with a massage chair at the basin and a coffee while you wait.

Which haircut should I book?

Book a Hair Cut with Blow Wave for a full service: shampoo and hair mask at the basin, your cut, and a blow wave or iron-styled finish. Choose Restyle if you’re changing your shape completely. A Dry Cut is a quick 20-minute trim to keep your current style sharp — no wash, no blow-dry — so arrive with freshly washed, dry hair. A Fringe Trim keeps you neat between appointments and welcomes walk-ins. Kids from Kindy to Year 6 have their own Kids’ Dry Cut, and men’s cuts, fades and buzz cuts have their own page.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a haircut at Hair Avenue?

A Hair Cut with Blow Wave is from $130 for short hair ($150 medium, $170 long, $185 extra long or thick, and $185 for a restyle) and includes a shampoo and conditioning treatment at the basin and a smooth blow wave finish; add the bouncy blow wave upgrade for $20. A dry cut is from $85, a fringe trim is $27, a kids’ dry cut is $45 and men’s cuts start from $40.

Do I need to wash my hair before a dry cut?

Yes, please. Dry cuts and fringe trims don’t include a shampoo or blow-dry, so arrive with freshly washed, dry hair worn the way you normally wear it. That lets your stylist see how your hair falls and cut it precisely.

Do you do curly haircuts?

Yes. Our stylists cut curls and waves dry, curl by curl, so each curl is shaped where it actually sits rather than stretched out wet and cut straight. Trin specialises in curly hair, and Elle is known for her dry curl-by-curl cuts.

Can I walk in for a haircut?

Walk-ins are welcome when there’s availability, and fringe trims are always walk-in friendly. For a cut and blow wave or a restyle, booking online guarantees your spot with the stylist you want.

How often should I get my hair cut?

Every six to eight weeks keeps a shaped style sharp. If you’re growing your hair, a light trim every ten to twelve weeks removes split ends without losing length.
