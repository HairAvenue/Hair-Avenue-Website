Frequently Asked Questions

Which days do the specials apply?

Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday. Book a package online for any of those days and the special price applies. On Saturdays our regular prices apply.

How does the 50% off haircut work?

When you book one of the colour, foils, balayage or smoothing packages Tuesday to Friday, you can add a haircut for $27.50 instead of the usual $55. Add it when you book, or ask your stylist on the day.

Can new clients book the specials?

Yes. The Tue–Fri specials are open to everyone, new and returning. If it’s your first colour with us, you’re welcome to book a free consultation first so we can check your hair and plan the appointment.

Do the prices depend on my hair length?

Yes. “From” prices are for short hair; medium, long and extra-long hair step up from there. Choose the length closest to yours when booking — our hair length guide shows what counts as short, medium or long — and your stylist will confirm the price before starting.

What if I’m not sure which package to choose?

Book a complimentary consultation. We’ll look at your hair, talk through what you want and recommend the package that gets you there, then you can book it for a Tuesday to Friday date.
