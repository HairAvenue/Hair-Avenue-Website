Proper men’s cuts, salon standards

A great men’s cut is about precision — clean graduation, sharp edges and a shape that still looks good three weeks later. At Hair Avenue our stylists bring full salon training to every clipper cut and fade, in a relaxed space on Lonsdale Street. No production line, no rushed finish: your cut is checked, refined and styled before you leave the chair.

Walk in, or book in seconds

We’re open six days including late nights Thursday and Friday until 8pm — easy to fit a cut around work. Walk-ins are always welcome when availability allows, and booking online takes under a minute. For fades and buzz cuts, arrive with clean hair (washed within 24 hours) so we can cut with precision.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

Both work. Walk-ins are welcome whenever a chair is free, and you can book online 24/7 to guarantee your time.

How often should I get a skin fade?

Most clients book every 2–4 weeks. A fade grows out fastest at the sides, so three weeks is the sweet spot for keeping it sharp.

Do you cut kids’ hair too?

Yes — kids from kindergarten to Year 6 are $45 for a dry cut, and family bookings are easy to arrange.

What’s included in the price?

Clipper cuts, fades and buzz cuts are haircut-only services — no shampoo or blow wave — which is how we keep the prices sharp too.
