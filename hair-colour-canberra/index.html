Your hair colour salon in Canberra

Hair Avenue is a hair colour salon on Lonsdale Street in Braddon, a few minutes from the centre of Canberra. Colour is at the heart of what we do: all over colour, root regrowth, toners and glosses, grey coverage, grey blending, balayage, foils and full blonde transformations.

We’re minutes from Civic, Turner, Reid, Ainslie, Dickson, Lyneham and O’Connor, and clients travel in from Gungahlin, Belconnen, Woden and Queanbeyan. Parking and the light rail are both close by.

Permanent colour, gloss or lightening: which do you need?

Most hair colour questions come down to these three. Many appointments combine two of them.

Permanent colour
  • Changes the colour of the hair itself, darker or lighter
  • Up to 100% grey coverage
  • Lifts natural hair by up to 5 levels
  • Does not wash out. It grows out, so roots need a regrowth every 4–6 weeks
  • All over colour from $229, regrowth from $199
Gloss or toner
  • A gentle colour that sits over the colour you have
  • No bleach, and it does not lighten
  • Takes out brassy yellow or orange and adds depth and shine
  • Fades softly over around 4–6 weeks, with no regrowth line
  • From $165 with a blow wave
Lightening
  • Foils, balayage or scalp bleach take hair lighter than colour can
  • The way to go lighter over old colour or box dye
  • Finished with a gloss or toner to set the shade
  • Half head foils from $319, balayage from $395 on weekdays
  • See balayage and our blonde specialists

Going darker, lighter or brighter

Going darker or richer. Chocolate, espresso, mocha or back to your natural shade: this is all over colour, with one even tone from roots to ends.

Coppers and reds. From soft rose copper to vivid red. These are the quickest shades to fade, so a gloss between colours keeps them bright.

Going lighter. Permanent colour can lift natural, uncoloured hair by up to five levels. It cannot lighten hair that already has colour in it, so going lighter over old colour or box dye means foils, balayage or a colour correction. That is work for our blonde specialists.

Fixing a colour. Box dye that went too dark, bands of different colour or patchy bleach are colour corrections. They are booked as a separate service, from $620 on weekdays or $670 on Saturdays, and removing old colour is quoted separately. Start with a 15-minute consultation ($35), where we look at your hair’s history and tell you what is achievable and how many visits it may take.

Colour backed by science

Koleston Perfect ME+ features Wella’s exclusive ME+ molecule, the result of seven years of research, which reduces the risk of developing new colour allergies by up to 60 times. Pure Balance Technology neutralises free radicals caused by mineral build-up, so you get up to 100% grey coverage, up to 5 levels of lift, and depth and shine that lasts.

We are a dedicated Wella salon. Permanent colour is Koleston Perfect ME+, lightening is done with Wella Blondor, and our glosses and toners are mixed from Wella professional colour, so your whole colour comes from one professional range.

Covering grey hair

Grey hair can be stubborn to colour, which is why it needs a permanent colour made for coverage. Koleston Perfect ME+ covers up to 100% of grey, and we match it to your natural shade so the result looks like your own hair.

Full coverage grows out with a line at the roots, so most clients book a root regrowth every 4–6 weeks. If you would rather soften your silver than hide it, grey blending uses fine highlights and a gloss and is refreshed every 8–12 weeks. Our guide, grey blending vs full coverage, compares the two.

What to expect at your hair colour appointment

  • Consultation — we look at your natural base, your colour history and any photos you bring, then choose the shade with you.
  • Mixing and application — your colour is mixed from Wella professional colour and applied to your roots, your lengths or both.
  • Processing — your colour develops while you relax with a coffee.
  • Shampoo at the basin — with a relaxing scalp massage in the all over colour package, and a hair mask or the Intense Mist treatment if you add one.
  • Blow wave or iron finish — styled so you can see the colour, with advice on looking after it at home.

How long you are in the chair depends on the service and your hair. A regrowth is quicker than an all over colour, and long or thick hair takes longer. Come with clean, dry hair if you can, bring a photo or two of the colour you have in mind, and tell us about any colour, bleach or box dye from the last 12 months.

How to make your hair colour last

  • First 48 hours — hold off washing so the colour settles.
  • Every wash — a sulphate-free shampoo for coloured hair, cooler water and heat protection before styling.
  • Once a week — a hydrating mask. Healthy hair holds colour and shine better.
  • Every 4–6 weeks — a regrowth to keep roots and grey covered, or a gloss if the tone has faded.
  • Sun, chlorine and hot tools — all fade colour faster. A hat in summer and a lower heat setting go a long way.

Hair colour: frequently asked questions

How much does hair colour cost in Canberra?

At Hair Avenue Braddon, all over colour is $229 for short hair, $249 for medium, $279 for long and $309 for extra long or thick hair, or from $210 on weekdays. A root regrowth is from $199 and a toner or gloss is from $165. Every price includes a blow wave or iron finish, and you can add a haircut for $65. If your hair needs extra product on the day, an additional $50 to $100 may apply.

What is included in an all over colour?

A consultation to choose your shade, the colour application using Wella professional colour, a relaxing scalp massage and shampoo, and a blow wave or iron finish. A haircut ($65) and the Intense Mist Hair Treatment ($20, normally $40) are optional add-ons.

What is the difference between all over colour and a regrowth?

All over colour covers your whole head from roots to ends, for a new shade, going darker or a full refresh. A root regrowth colours only the new growth at your roots and blends it into the colour you already have, so it is quicker and costs less (from $199). If your lengths have gone warm or dull as well, the Regrowth + All Over Toner package (from $230) refreshes both.

How often should I have my hair coloured?

Permanent colour does not wash out, but your roots grow, so most clients book a regrowth every 4–6 weeks. A toner or gloss lasts around 4–6 weeks. Softer, blended colour goes longer between visits: grey blending is refreshed every 8–12 weeks and balayage every 12–16 weeks.

Do you cover grey hair?

Yes. Wella Koleston Perfect ME+ gives up to 100% grey coverage, matched to your natural shade. If you would rather soften your grey than cover it, ask about grey blending, which uses fine highlights and a gloss and grows out with no hard line.

What is the difference between permanent colour and a gloss or toner?

Permanent colour changes the colour of the hair itself. It covers grey, can lift natural hair by up to five levels and stays until it grows out. A gloss or toner is a gentle, bleach-free colour that sits over the colour you have. It does not lighten your hair: it changes the tone, takes out brassiness and adds shine, then fades softly over around 4–6 weeks.

Can I go lighter with an all over colour?

On natural, uncoloured hair, permanent colour can lift by up to five levels. It cannot lighten hair that already has colour in it. To go lighter over old colour or box dye you need lightening, such as foils or balayage, or a colour correction. If you are not sure, book a 15-minute consultation ($35) first.

Can you fix box dye or a colour that went wrong?

Yes. Colour correction is a separate service, from $620 on weekdays or $670 on Saturdays, and removing old colour is quoted separately. Book a 15-minute consultation ($35) first: we look at your hair’s history and condition and tell you what is achievable and how many visits it may take.

Will colouring damage my hair?

We colour with Wella Koleston Perfect ME+, whose Pure Balance Technology gives truer colour with significantly less damage, colour after colour. You can add the Intense Mist Hair Treatment for $20 with an all over colour or a regrowth to put moisture back in. If your hair is not ready for the colour you want yet, we will tell you and give you a plan to get it there.

Do I need a consultation before colouring?

For all over colour, regrowth and toner you can book straight in online, and choosing your shade is part of the appointment. For a big change, going lighter or a colour correction, or if you are not sure what to book, book a 15-minute consultation ($35) first. Consultations are available Monday to Friday.

Are there midweek specials on hair colour?

Yes. On weekdays, all over colour is from $210: short $210, medium $234, long $269 and extra long or thick $299, and you can add a haircut for $45 (normally $65). All our midweek offers are on the Weekday Specials page.

Which hair colour brand do you use?

Wella Professionals exclusively. Our permanent colour is Koleston Perfect ME+, and our glosses and toners are mixed from Wella professional colour.
