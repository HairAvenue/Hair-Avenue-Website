The blow wave that lasts for days

A blow wave at Hair Avenue starts at the basin: a luxurious shampoo and scalp massage in the massage chair, then a Wella hair mask or conditioner matched to your hair type. Your stylist then dries and shapes your hair with a round brush or thermal styling for a smooth, shiny finish full of movement — styled the way you actually want to wear it.

Every blow wave is done by an experienced stylist, never an apprentice. Elle, our blow wave specialist, is known for smooth, bouncy finishes that hold their shape for days, whether it’s for a wedding, a night out or simply because you deserve it.

Classic or bouncy?

A classic blow wave gives you sleek, polished hair with soft movement — the everyday blow-dry done properly. A bouncy blow wave is set with a round brush for big, soft volume and curl through the lengths, the one that turns heads at weddings, birthdays, race days and photos. Going somewhere special? Choose the bouncy blow wave with rollers or styling: we set your hair in rollers and finish with styling, for fuller volume that holds far longer than a standard blow-dry.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a blow wave in Canberra?

At Hair Avenue Braddon a classic blow wave is $85 for short hair, $105 medium, $120 long and $135 extra long, including a shampoo, scalp massage and hair mask. A bouncy blow wave is from $109, or $160 with rollers or styling for events. Tuesday to Friday, blow waves start from $79 with the Intense Treatment Mask included free.

How long does a blow wave last?

Usually two to four days, depending on your hair type, the weather and how you treat it at night. A bouncy blow wave set with rollers holds its volume longer, which is why we recommend it for weddings and events.

Do I need to arrive with clean hair?

No. Every blow wave includes a shampoo and scalp massage at the basin and a hair mask or conditioner, so come as you are. If you’d like the deeper Intense Mist treatment, add it when you book for $20 instead of $40.

Can you blow wave hair with extensions?

Yes. Choose the extensions option when you book so we can allow the extra time and gentle handling tape-in extensions need. Our stylists work with Great Lengths extensions every day.

How do I make my blow wave last longer?

Skip washing for a couple of days, use a dry shampoo at the roots if they feel flat, sleep on a silk pillowcase or with your hair in a loose, high bun, and keep it away from steam in the bathroom. Ask your stylist for a finishing product to suit your hair when you’re in.
