Official Great Lengths Salon

Tape-in hair extensions with Great Lengths Essenza tapes — about 20% smaller and lighter than standard tapes, with a medical-grade hypoallergenic adhesive and clean, residue-free removal. Colour-matched to your own hair, moved up every 6–8 weeks and reusable for months. Every Hair Avenue stylist is Great Lengths–trained.

Extensions Consultation & Colour MatchBook this first, before any install$35Book

A relaxed, no-obligation chat with a Great Lengths–trained stylist to work out whether tape-ins suit your hair, what you’d need, and exactly how it will look. Allow about 15 minutes.

What’s included: a check of your hair’s condition and density; a plan for how many pieces you need and where they’ll sit — a full head for length, a partial set for volume, or a few single-sided pieces around the face; and a colour match against your own hair in natural daylight at the root, mid-lengths and ends, from the Great Lengths Essenza range including rooted blends. You leave with an exact quote and a booking plan.

How to prepare: come with your hair down, clean and dry, styled the way you usually wear it, and bring a photo or two of the length or fullness you’re after. If you already wear extensions, leave them in so we can see them.

Why it matters: no guesswork. If your hair isn’t ready for extensions yet, we’ll tell you and give you a plan to get it there — we’d rather say so now than after.

Good to know: all installs using salon-supplied Great Lengths hair must be booked with a consultation first, so we can match and order your shades before your fitting.

New Tape Extension InstallGreat Lengths tapes fitted in one visit$270 – $570Book

Longer, fuller hair in a single appointment. Great Lengths Essenza tapes are about 20% smaller and lighter than standard tapes, so they hide easily and move like your own hair. Allow one to two and a half hours, depending on the number of pieces.

What’s included: fitting of your Great Lengths hair, or your own hair if it’s in good condition and safe to apply; cutting and blending so the ends sit seamlessly with yours; a styled finish; and aftercare advice so you know exactly how to look after them at home.

How we do it: a small section of your hair is sandwiched between two slim tapes with a medical-grade, hypoallergenic adhesive. The tapes lie flat, are placed to follow your natural growth pattern and sit clear of your parting and hairline, so nothing shows whether you wear your hair down or in a low style. No heat, no glue bonds.

How to prepare: arrive with clean, dry hair washed with shampoo only — no conditioner, oils or styling product near the roots, so the tapes bond properly. Booking colour as well? We’ll do the colour first so your extensions are matched to the fresh shade.

Why clients love it: the quickest professional extension method to fit, light and comfortable for everyday wear, and the hair is reusable — it’s simply moved up every 6–8 weeks as your own hair grows.

Tape Extension Move UpMaintenance every 6 to 8 weeks$280 – $580Book

Your regular maintenance appointment, every 6 to 8 weeks. As your hair grows the tapes grow down with it, so we bring them back up to the root and your extensions look freshly fitted again. Allow one to two and a half hours.

What’s included: gentle removal of every tape, fresh tape tabs on the same hair, re-application at the root, a shampoo and blow wave to finish, and a quick health check of both your own hair and the extension hair. Removal and blow wave are part of the package — nothing to book separately.

How we do it: a specialised Great Lengths remover dissolves the adhesive so each tape releases without pulling. The hair is cleaned of any residue, re-taped, then fitted in fresh sections so the weight is spread evenly and your own hair keeps growing out healthy.

How to prepare: come with clean, dry hair, shampoo only, with no conditioner or oils near the tapes. There’s nothing to take out beforehand — it all happens in the one visit. Try not to stretch past 8 weeks, as grown-out tapes can tangle and pull.

Why clients love it: the same Great Lengths hair is reused visit after visit, the result stays consistent, and your natural hair is protected. Wearing extensions from another salon? We’re happy to move those up too — if the hair is in good condition we’ll re-tape it, and if it isn’t we’ll tell you honestly what your options are.

Common Questions ▾

Will they damage my hair? Not when applied and removed correctly — the tapes are light and flat, and removal is residue-free.

Do they suit fine hair? Often, yes. The smaller tapes lie flat and hide well; we check density at your consultation.

Can I wear my hair up? Low ponytails and soft styles are ideal. Tell us if you love high styles and we’ll plan placement for it.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit.

Show FAQsHide FAQs
Is Hair Avenue walk-in friendly? +
Yes — while we recommend booking online to guarantee your preferred time and stylist, walk-ins are always welcome when availability allows. We'll do our best to fit you in — we've even been known to squeeze in last-minute visitors from out of town.
Where can I park near Hair Avenue in Braddon? +
Free street parking is available along Lonsdale Street and surrounding streets in the Braddon precinct. There's also paid parking at the nearby Braddon car park. We're just off Northbourne Avenue, a short five-minute walk from Canberra Centre and Civic. The salon is at Unit 102/16 Lonsdale Street — look for us at street level.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), EFTPOS, and contactless payments including Apple Pay and Google Pay. We want to make payment as easy as possible.
Do you offer a consultation before colour services? +
Yes. A 15-minute consultation with one of our stylists is $35, including the hair extension consultation and colour match, and the fee is charged on the day. It's the perfect opportunity to discuss your vision, assess your hair's condition and get an exact quote. Book it through our online booking system.
Is Hair Avenue LGBTQ+ friendly? +
100%. Hair Avenue is a proudly inclusive, LGBTQ+ friendly salon. Everyone is welcome and celebrated here, exactly as they are. Our team is committed to creating a safe, comfortable, and affirming space for all clients.
How do I book an appointment? +
You can book online 24/7 through our Phorest booking system — just click any "Book Now" button on our website. You can also call us directly at 0449 801 937, or simply walk in! Online booking lets you choose your preferred stylist, service, date, and time.
What is Hair SmoothFiller and how long does it last? +
Hair SmoothFiller is a deep conditioning treatment that infuses your hair with proteins and vitamins, repairing damage, eliminating frizz, and restoring incredible shine. It's formaldehyde-free and safe for colour-treated hair. Results typically last 8-12 weeks. It's one of our most popular services and something most Canberra salons don't offer.
What is nanoplasty? +
Nanoplasty is an advanced smoothing treatment that uses nanotechnology to penetrate deep into the hair fibre, eliminating frizz and adding lasting shine without harsh chemicals. It's ideal for curly, wavy, or unruly hair types. The treatment is gentle enough for most hair and delivers silky, manageable results that last for months.
Do you do men's haircuts? +
Absolutely — men's haircuts are a core part of what we do. Clipper cuts ($65), skin fades ($65) and buzz cuts ($40), plus full restyles. Walk-ins are welcome when availability allows, or book online in seconds.
Do you do kids' haircuts? +
Yes — we welcome children and families. Our team creates a relaxed, fun environment so kids feel comfortable. We understand little ones can be wriggly — no judgement here. We're also a great choice for a family booking so everyone can get their hair done together.
Do you do hair extensions? +
Yes — Hair Avenue is an official Great Lengths salon, and every stylist on our team is Great Lengths–trained in tape-in extensions. Start with a 15-minute consultation and colour match ($35), where we check your hair, plan the pieces and give you an exact quote. New installs, move-ups every 6–8 weeks and removals can all be booked online — and we happily move up or remove extensions from other salons. Learn more about tape extensions in Canberra›
Do you have blonde specialists in Canberra? +
Yes — blonde is what we do most. Krystle and Trin are blonde specialists, and every foil, balayage and toner uses Wella Blondor with built-in bond protection for up to 97% less breakage. Half-head foils are from $299, a full head from $350 and balayage from $375 Tuesday to Friday or $395 on Saturdays, all including a custom toner and blow wave, with midweek packages from $275.
