Why choose balayage?

Balayage is a freehand colour technique where lightener is painted onto the surface of the hair for a soft, natural gradient — brighter through the ends, seamlessly blended at the root. Because there's no hard regrowth line, it's one of the lowest-maintenance colour services you can choose: most clients refresh every 3–4 months instead of every 6 weeks. It suits almost every base colour and length, and our stylists tailor placement to your face shape and how you wear your hair.

Types of balayage we do

“Balayage” covers a family of hand-painted techniques. Your stylist will recommend one, or blend two, based on your base colour, the brightness you want and how often you’d like to be back in the chair.

Classic & lived-in balayage

Soft, sun-kissed lightness painted through the mid-lengths and ends with a blurred root. Refresh every 12–16 weeks.

Blonde balayage

Bright, creamy or icy blonde ends over a natural base. The most popular balayage in the salon, paired with a custom toner.

Brunette, caramel & bronde

Warm caramel, honey or bronde ribbons that add dimension to dark hair without turning it blonde.

Foilyage

Hand-painted pieces wrapped in foil for extra lift — the softness of balayage with the brightness of foils.

Money piece & face-frame

Bold, bright pieces around the face for instant impact. A quick add-on or a standalone refresh.

Reverse balayage & root smudge

Depth painted back in for over-lightened hair, or a shadow root that makes any balayage grow out for months.

Prefer a lighter touch? A partial balayage concentrates the colour through the top and face-framing sections for a subtler change at a lower price — ask at your consultation.

What to expect at your balayage appointment

  • Consultation — we look at your base colour, hair history and inspiration photos, then agree the placement, tone and price.
  • Sectioning and hand-painting — lightener is painted onto the surface of each section, heavier through the ends and feathered towards the root.
  • Processing with bond protection — Wella Blondor with Wellaplex works while you relax with a coffee.
  • Rinse, toner and treatment — a custom gloss or toner sets the exact shade, then a treatment restores softness.
  • Blow wave or iron finish — styled so you can see the movement, with a home-care plan to keep it bright.

Allow 2.5–4 hours in total. Arrive with clean, dry hair washed a day or two before, and bring a photo or two of the result you have in mind.

How to keep balayage looking fresh

  • First 48 hours — hold off washing so the toner settles, then switch to a sulphate-free shampoo and always use heat protection.
  • Every week — one purple or blue toning wash for blondes, a hydrating mask for everyone.
  • Every 6–8 weeks — a toner or gloss to keep the tone true and the shine high.
  • Every 8–12 weeks — optional Blonde Refresh of 20 foils if you like it brighter around the face.
  • Every 12–16 weeks — your balayage repainted to lift the new growth and rebalance the blend.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does balayage take?

Allow 2.5–4 hours depending on hair length, thickness and how much lift we're creating. Ultra blonde and corrective work can take longer — we'll give you an accurate time at your free consultation.

How often do I need to redo balayage?

Most clients book a refresh every 12–16 weeks. Because the colour is hand-painted with a soft root blend, it grows out without a harsh line.

Will balayage damage my hair?

We use Wella Blondor with Wellaplex bond protection, which reduces breakage by up to 97% compared to traditional lightening. Your stylist will assess your hair's condition first and recommend the safest path to your goal.

Do you offer a free balayage consultation?

Yes — colour consultations are complimentary. We'll assess your hair, discuss tones and placement, and give you an exact quote before you commit.

Midweek (Tuesday to Friday) the Balayage Special Deal is from $385 and includes 50% off a haircut. The final price depends on your hair's length, thickness and starting colour, and you'll get an exact quote at your free consultation.

Does balayage work on dark or brunette hair?

Beautifully. Caramel, honey and bronde balayage add dimension to dark hair without turning it blonde, and a shadow root keeps the grow-out soft. If you want a very light blonde result on a dark base, we may plan it over two visits so the hair stays healthy.

Can I have balayage on short hair?

Yes. Bobs and lobs suit balayage particularly well — we adjust the placement so the lightness sits where it frames your face and catches the light, rather than only on the ends.

Can I get balayage over box dye or previously coloured hair?

Usually, but it needs a careful assessment first. Box dye and old colour lift unevenly, so your stylist may recommend a colour-correction approach, a strand test or a two-step plan. Bring photos of your hair history to the consultation.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Come with clean, dry hair washed a day or two before, wear your hair the way you normally do so we can see how it falls, and bring inspiration photos. If you're also booking a haircut, the colour goes first so the cut shows off the finished blend.

Can I add a haircut to my balayage?

Any day of the week you can add a cut and blow wave when you book. Tuesday to Friday, the Balayage Special Deal includes 50% off your haircut.
