What is grey blending?

Instead of covering every silver strand with solid colour, grey blending works with your grey. Fine highlights or lowlights are woven through your hair, then a demi-permanent gloss melts everything together — softening the contrast between silver and your natural or coloured tone. The result is dimensional, natural-looking and effortlessly stylish.

Because nothing is flatly “covered”, there’s no hard line as your hair grows. The blend simply grows out softly, which makes grey blending one of the lowest-maintenance colour services you can choose — and the kindest way to transition towards your natural silver.

Who it’s for

Grey blending is perfect if you’re seeing your first silvers and want to soften rather than hide them, if you’re growing out years of colour and want the journey to look intentional, or if you love your grey and simply want it polished, dimensional and deliberate.

What’s the difference between grey blending and full coverage?

Full coverage colours every strand for a single, solid shade — beautiful, but it needs a regrowth touch-up every 4–6 weeks as the line appears. Grey blending softens the silver instead of hiding it, so it grows out gently with no hard line. Read our full comparison guide.

How often will I need a refresh?

Most grey blending clients refresh every 8–12 weeks, depending on how fast your hair grows and how much contrast you like. That’s roughly half the salon visits of full-coverage maintenance.

Can grey blending help me go fully grey?

Yes — it’s the most graceful route. Blending bridges your existing colour and your incoming silver so every stage of the grow-out looks intentional and polished, rather than half-done.
