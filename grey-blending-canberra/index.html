What is grey blending?

Instead of covering every silver strand with solid colour, grey blending works with your grey. Fine highlights or lowlights are woven through your hair, then a demi-permanent gloss melts everything together — softening the contrast between silver and your natural or coloured tone. The result is dimensional, natural-looking and effortlessly stylish.

Because nothing is flatly “covered”, there’s no hard line as your hair grows. The blend simply grows out softly, which makes grey blending one of the lowest-maintenance colour services you can choose — and the kindest way to transition towards your natural silver.

Who it’s for

Grey blending is perfect if you’re seeing your first silvers and want to soften rather than hide them, if you’re growing out years of colour and want the journey to look intentional, or if you love your grey and simply want it polished, dimensional and deliberate.

Ways we blend grey

Grey blending is a family of techniques, and most blends use two or three of them together. Your stylist chooses the mix from how much grey you have, where it sits and the colour you already wear.

Fine highlights

Very fine foils of lightness through the part and hairline, so silver strands read as highlights and the regrowth line softens.

Lowlights

Soft depth woven back in, close to your natural shade. Ideal when you’re mostly grey and want dimension without going back to solid colour.

Demi-permanent gloss

Sheer, bleach-free colour that tones your grey and melts everything together. It fades gradually, so there’s no hard line as it wears off.

Face-framing blend

Extra softness around the hairline and temples, where grey usually shows first and catches the light most.

Growing out to natural silver

Your old colour is broken up and blended into the silver coming through, so each stage of the grow-out looks deliberate.

Silver polish

Already grey and loving it? A toner takes out yellow tones and adds shine, so your silver looks bright and cared for.

Grey blending on dark hair

Grey shows most on dark brown and black hair, because the contrast between silver and your base is at its sharpest. That’s also where blending makes the biggest difference.

On a dark base we usually combine very fine highlights around the part and hairline with lowlights close to your natural shade, then tone with a gloss. You stay a brunette: the lighter pieces simply break up the solid dark, so the silver has something to blend into.

Going lighter from a dark base is booked as a first-time grey blend ($575 for short to medium hair, $670 for long or thick hair), because it takes longer and uses more product. If there’s box dye or old dark colour in your hair, removing it is quoted separately, so book a 15-minute consultation ($35) first and we’ll plan it properly. On very dark hair the softest blend can take more than one visit.

What to expect at your grey blending appointment

  • Consultation — we look at how much grey you have, where it sits and your colour history, then agree the blend, the tone and the price before anything is mixed.
  • Foils and colour — fine highlights or lowlights are woven through, or demi-permanent colour is applied, depending on your plan.
  • Processing — your colour develops while you relax with a coffee.
  • Rinse, gloss and WellaPlex — a gloss or toner sets the tone, and the WellaPlex bond builder goes on at the basin to help rebuild strength.
  • Blow wave or iron finish — styled so you can see the blend, with advice on keeping it fresh at home.

Your first grey blend is the longest visit, because the whole blend is set up from scratch. Plan for a few hours in the chair; follow-up visits are quicker. Bring a photo or two of the result you have in mind, and tell us about any box dye or old colour.

How to keep your grey blend looking fresh

  • Every wash — a sulphate-free shampoo and heat protection, so the gloss lasts.
  • Once a week — a purple shampoo to keep silver bright and stop it yellowing.
  • Between blends — a toner or gloss (from $165) if the tone fades. A gloss lasts around 4–6 weeks.
  • Every 8–12 weeks — your grey blend refreshed, priced by length from $440.

What is the difference between grey blending and full coverage?

Full coverage colours every strand for a single, solid shade. It looks polished, but it needs a regrowth touch-up every 4–6 weeks as the line appears. Grey blending softens the silver instead of hiding it, so it grows out gently with no hard line. Our guide to grey blending vs full coverage compares the two in detail.

Does grey blending cover grey completely?

No, and it is not meant to. Grey blending softens the contrast between your silver and the rest of your hair, so the grey looks intentional. If you want no grey showing at all, full coverage with a regrowth colour is the better choice, and we will tell you so at your consultation.

Does grey blending work on dark hair?

Yes. Dark hair is where blending makes the biggest difference, because the contrast with silver is sharpest. We combine very fine highlights around the part and hairline with lowlights close to your natural shade, then tone with a gloss. Going lighter from a dark base is booked as a first-time grey blend ($575 or $670 depending on length), and on very dark hair the softest result can take more than one visit.

How long does grey blending last?

Most grey blending clients refresh every 8–12 weeks, depending on how fast your hair grows and how much contrast you like. That is roughly half the salon visits of full-coverage maintenance. The gloss fades gradually over around 4–6 weeks, so some clients book a toner or gloss (from $165) between blends.

How long does a grey blending appointment take?

Your first grey blend is the longest visit, because the whole blend is set up from scratch, so plan for a few hours in the chair including the gloss and blow wave. Follow-up visits are quicker. The exact time depends on your hair length and thickness and how much grey is being blended.

Will grey blending damage my hair?

Grey blending is one of the gentler colour services. Lightener is only used on fine, selected strands, the gloss is demi-permanent and bleach-free, and every grey blend includes the WellaPlex bond builder at the basin to help rebuild strength from the inside out.

Can grey blending help me go fully grey?

Yes, it is the most graceful route. Blending bridges your existing colour and your incoming silver so every stage of the grow-out looks intentional and polished, rather than half-done.
