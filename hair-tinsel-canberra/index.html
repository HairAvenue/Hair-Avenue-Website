What is hair tinsel?

Hair tinsel (sometimes called fairy hair) is fine, shimmering strands tied individually to your own hair. Each strand is feather-light — you won’t feel it — and because it’s knotted rather than glued, it’s completely gentle on your hair.

Choose a colour that glitters against your own shade — silver, gold, rainbow brights and more — and decide how much sparkle you want: a few subtle strands that flash when the light catches, or a full head of shimmer.

Perfect for…

Birthday treats, school formals and dance concerts, festivals and race day, holiday sparkle — or simply making an ordinary week more fun. It’s one of our most-loved add-ons for kids, and just as popular with adults booking it alongside a cut or blow wave.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does hair tinsel cost in Canberra?

At Hair Avenue Braddon, hair tinsel is $60 — and we offer a dedicated kids’ service for $45. Both include a range of colours and gentle professional application.

How long does hair tinsel last?

Each strand is tied to one of your own hairs, so it stays until that hair naturally sheds — typically several weeks with gentle care. You can also have strands removed earlier at any time, without damage.

Does hair tinsel damage your hair?

No. The strands are lightweight and knotted in rather than glued, and our gentle application is kind even to young hair — which is why we offer a dedicated kids’ service.

Can you wash and style hair with tinsel in?

Yes — wash, brush and style as normal. Just be gentle around the strands and keep hot tools on a low setting near them.

Do you do hair tinsel for kids?

Yes! Our kids’ hair tinsel service is $45, with a safe, gentle application designed especially for children who love a bit of extra shimmer.
