Frequently Asked Questions

How do I buy a Hair Avenue gift voucher?

Online, in about a minute. Choose an amount, add the recipient’s name and your message, and pay by card. The voucher is emailed straight away, to them or to you, so it works even as a last-minute gift. You can also buy one at reception in the salon.

What can the voucher be used for?

Anything at Hair Avenue Braddon: cuts, colour, balayage and foils, blonde services, smoothing treatments, blow waves, upstyles, hair tinsel, extensions and the professional products we stock.

Can the voucher be used over more than one visit?

Yes. If a service costs less than the voucher, the remaining balance stays on it for next time.

How long is the voucher valid for?

Three years from the date of purchase, in line with Australian gift card rules.

How do I use a voucher when booking?

Book as normal online, by phone or as a walk-in, then show the voucher code at the salon when you pay. If you would like to book a specific stylist, just mention it when you book.
