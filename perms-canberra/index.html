Not your 1980s perm

Modern perming has come a long way. Instead of one-size-fits-all tight curls, today’s techniques let your stylist design the exact movement you want — loose, romantic beach waves, bouncy defined curls, or simply lasting body and volume in hair that falls flat by lunchtime.

At Hair Avenue we tailor the curl size, solution and technique to your hair type and the look you’re after, then finish with professional Wella care. The result grows out gracefully: the wave softens gradually as your hair grows, with no harsh line.

Which perm is right for you?

A classic perm transforms your whole style with curls or waves. A root perm lifts just the first few centimetres for volume without changing your lengths — a favourite for fine, flat hair. And if you want the opposite of curl, our Shiseido permanent straightening delivers sleek, straight hair that lasts. Not sure? Start with a free consultation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a perm cost in Canberra?

At Hair Avenue Braddon, our classic perm starts from $169 (available Tuesday to Friday). Root perms start from $199, perms for short hair are $219, extra-long hair is $349, and a fringe-only perm is $75. Every perm includes a consultation — or book a free consultation first if you’re unsure.

How long does a perm last?

Most perms last several months. The curl softens gradually as your hair grows, so there’s no harsh grow-out line — the wave simply relaxes over time. Longevity depends on your hair type, the curl size you choose and your home care.

What is a root perm?

A root perm applies the solution only at the roots, creating lift and volume where it matters without changing your lengths. It’s perfect for reviving flat, limp hair — or maintaining volume between full perms.

Can I perm coloured hair?

Often, yes — but it depends on your hair’s condition. We assess your hair first and plan the order and timing between colour and perm services at your consultation, so your hair stays healthy.

Will a perm damage my hair?

Modern perm techniques are far gentler than the perms of decades past. We check your hair’s condition before we begin, tailor the solution and curl size to your hair, and finish with professional Wella care to keep it soft and healthy.
