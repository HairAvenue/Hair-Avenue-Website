What are tape-in hair extensions?

Tape-in extensions are the fastest professional way to add length or volume. A small section of your own hair is sandwiched between two slim wefts of real hair, each with a fine adhesive strip, so the tapes lie completely flat against the scalp. The result is seamless and natural in movement, comfortable enough to forget about, and quick to fit compared with strand-by-strand methods.

As your hair grows the tapes move with it, so every 6–8 weeks we take them out, re-tape them and move them back up to the root. The same Great Lengths hair is reused each time, which makes tapes the lower-commitment, better-value way into extensions — with regular salon support built in.

Length, volume or just a little more

A full head for dramatic length, a partial set for thickness through the ends, or a few single-sided pieces around the face for a soft, feathered finish — tape extensions can be tailored to exactly what your hair needs. At your consultation we plan the number of pieces, the placement and the shade, then confirm your price before anything goes in.

Aftercare & living with tapes

  • Wash gently, dry thoroughly. Use extension-safe, sulphate-free care and make sure the tape area is fully dry at the roots after every wash.
  • Keep oils away from the tapes. Oil-based, silicone-heavy or alcohol-based products can soften the adhesive — apply conditioner and oils from mid-lengths to ends only.
  • Mind Canberra’s dry seasons. Cold, dry winters and hot summers pull moisture from hair, so keep up a weekly extension-safe mask from mid-lengths to ends and let the tape area dry fully after every wash.
  • Brush from the ends up. Hold the roots and work knots out gradually with a soft brush, and sleep with your hair in a loose plait.
  • Swim smart. Tie your hair back, rinse with fresh water straight after and dry the tapes well.
  • Book your move-up at 6–8 weeks. Regular maintenance keeps the tapes hidden, prevents tangling and protects your own hair as it grows.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do tape-in hair extensions last?

The tapes are moved back up to the root every 6–8 weeks as your hair grows. The Great Lengths hair itself can be re-taped and reused up to three times with proper care, so one set lasts many months.

Are tape-in extensions damaging to your hair?

Not when they’re applied and removed correctly by a trained stylist. Great Lengths tapes are flat and lightweight so the weight is spread evenly, the adhesive is medical-grade and hypoallergenic, and removal uses a specialised solution that dissolves the adhesive with no pulling and no residue.

Are tape extensions suitable for fine or thin hair?

Often, yes. Great Lengths tapes are around 20% smaller than standard tapes and lie flat against the scalp, so they can suit fine to medium hair. We check density and condition at your consultation and place the tapes where they stay hidden.

Tape-in, keratin bond or clip-in extensions: which is best?

Tape-ins are the quickest to fit and the easiest to live with: applied in about an hour, moved up every 6–8 weeks, and the same hair is reused. Keratin bond (strand-by-strand) extensions take several hours to apply, cost more up front and stay in for 3–5 months before removal, but they allow higher, tighter updos. Clip-ins are temporary pieces you put in and take out yourself. At Hair Avenue we fit Great Lengths tape-ins — and if another method would suit your hair or lifestyle better, we’ll tell you at your free consultation.

How long does the appointment take?

The free consultation and colour match takes about 20 minutes. Allow around 1 hour for an install or move-up of up to 10 pieces and up to 2.5 hours for larger sets. Removal on its own takes about 15–30 minutes, or longer with a blow wave.

Can I wear my hair up with tape extensions?

Low ponytails and soft, relaxed styles are ideal. High or tight styles can expose the tapes, so if you love wearing your hair up, tell us at your consultation and we’ll plan placement with that in mind.

Can I swim and exercise with tape-in extensions?

Yes. Tie your hair back, rinse with fresh water straight after swimming and dry the tape area thoroughly. Keep oils, silicone-heavy and alcohol-based products away from the tapes.
