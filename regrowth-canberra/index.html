What is a root regrowth?

A regrowth is colour on the new hair at your roots only. As your hair grows, a line appears between the new growth and your coloured lengths, and any grey shows first along your part and hairline. A regrowth colours that band and blends it into the rest, so your colour looks even again from roots to ends. You might know it as a root touch-up, a root tint or a retouch.

Why regular regrowth keeps colour looking its best

The line usually becomes visible around the 4–6 week mark for all over colour. Staying on a regular regrowth schedule is gentler on your hair than letting the roots grow out and re-colouring the whole head each time, because your lengths are not coloured again and again. It also keeps your look polished for work, special occasions, or simply feeling like yourself.

Covering grey at the roots

Grey shows first where everyone looks: along your part, at your hairline and at your temples. Every regrowth uses Wella Koleston Perfect ME+, which delivers up to 100% grey coverage with rich, true-to-tone results and significantly less damage. We match it to your existing colour, so the join does not show.

Hair grows about a centimetre a month, which is why most clients covering grey book every 4–6 weeks. If you would rather be in the salon less often, grey blending softens the grey instead of covering it and is refreshed every 8–12 weeks. Our guide, grey blending vs full coverage, compares the two.

How often to book

  • Every 4–6 weeks — regrowth for all over colour and grey coverage.
  • Every 6–8 weeks — scalp bleach regrowth for blondes.
  • When your lengths fade — choose the regrowth with the all over toner, so roots and ends are refreshed in one visit.

What to expect at your regrowth appointment

  • A quick check — we look at your regrowth, your grey and how your lengths are holding their colour.
  • Colour matched and applied — your shade is mixed from Wella professional colour and applied to the new growth only.
  • Processing — your colour develops while you relax with a coffee.
  • Shampoo at the basin — and, if you chose it, the toner through your mid-lengths and ends with a hair mask.
  • Blow wave or iron finish — styled so your colour looks freshly done from roots to ends.

A regrowth is quicker than an all over colour, because only the new growth is coloured. If your colour was done elsewhere, tell us what was used if you know, and mention any box dye.

Keeping your colour fresh between visits

  • Every wash — a sulphate-free shampoo for coloured hair, cooler water and heat protection before styling.
  • Once a week — a hydrating mask. Healthy hair holds colour and shine better.
  • Skip the box dye — colour over colour builds up in bands, which makes your next regrowth harder to match.
  • Book your next one before you leave — a regrowth works best on a steady 4–6 week rhythm.

Regrowth colour: frequently asked questions

What is a root regrowth?

A root regrowth, also called a root touch-up or root tint, is colour on the new hair at your roots only. It is matched to the shade you already have and blended in, so your colour looks even again from roots to ends and any grey is covered.

How much does a regrowth colour cost in Canberra?

At Hair Avenue Braddon the Regrowth Package is $199 for short hair, $235 for medium, $270 for long and $285 for extra long. Regrowth + All Over Toner is $230, $250 or $275, and Scalp Bleach Regrowth + Toner is $320, $350 or $395. Every price includes a blow wave, and you can add a haircut for $65. Thick hair needs more product, so an additional $50 to $100 may apply.

How often should I get my regrowth done?

Most clients book every 4–6 weeks for all over colour and grey coverage, and every 6–8 weeks for a blonde scalp bleach regrowth. We’ll recommend a schedule based on how fast your hair grows and how visible your roots are.

What is the difference between a regrowth and a full colour?

A regrowth colours only the new growth at your roots and blends it into your existing colour, so it is quicker and gentler than re-colouring the whole head. All over colour goes from roots to ends, for a new shade, going darker or a full refresh. If your lengths have faded too, the Regrowth + All Over Toner package refreshes both.

Does the Regrowth Package include a toner?

The Regrowth Package is root colour, a shampoo and a blow wave or iron finish. If the rest of your hair needs toning as well, book the Regrowth + All Over Toner package (from $230), which adds a toner from the mid-lengths to the ends.

Do you cover greys?

Yes. Wella Koleston Perfect ME+ delivers up to 100% grey coverage. If you’d prefer to soften your grey rather than fully cover it, ask us about grey blending instead.

What is a scalp bleach regrowth?

It is the regrowth service for blondes. Your roots are lifted with lightener and then toned, so they blend back into the blonde you already have. It is for regrowth maintenance only, is usually booked every 6–8 weeks, and starts from $320 including the toner, a treatment mask and a blow wave.

Can you do my regrowth if my colour was done somewhere else?

Usually, yes. We match your regrowth to the colour you have. If the colour is uneven, was done with box dye, or you want to change shade, book a 15-minute consultation ($35) first, because a regrowth is not suitable for colour correction.

Do I need a consultation first?

For regular regrowth maintenance you can book straight in. For a first visit, or if you’re changing shade, a 15-minute consultation ($35) lets us match your colour precisely. Consultations are available Monday to Friday.

Can I add a haircut to my regrowth?

Yes. Add a haircut for $65 when you book. You can also add the Intense Mist Hair Treatment for $20 (normally $40) with the Regrowth Package or the Scalp Bleach Regrowth.
