How Much Does Grey Blending Cost?

By Hair Avenue Braddon · Canberra prices, updated November 2026

At Hair Avenue Braddon in Canberra, grey blending costs $440 to $565 for a refresh, depending on your hair length, and $575 or $670 for your first grey blend. Every price includes the gloss or toner, the WellaPlex bond builder and a blow wave.

What the price includes

Grey blending at Hair Avenue is one package price. It covers:

  • the fine highlights, lowlights or demi-permanent colour your blend needs
  • a gloss or toner to set the tone
  • the WellaPlex bond builder at the basin
  • a blow wave

Many salons quote these as separate parts: the foils, then a toner, then a blow-dry. When you compare prices, check what is included.

Why your first grey blend costs more

The first visit sets up the whole blend from scratch, so it takes longer and uses more product. Book the first-time option if you are starting grey blending with us, going lighter from a dark base or correcting uneven colour. After that you pay the refresh price for your hair length.

What can change the price

  • Thickness. If your hair needs extra product on the day, an additional $50 to $100 may apply.
  • Old colour. Removing box dye or old dark colour is not part of grey blending and is quoted separately.
  • A haircut. Add one for $65 when you book.

Not sure what your hair needs? A 15-minute consultation ($35), available Monday to Friday, gives you a clear plan and a quote.

Is grey blending expensive?

Per visit, it costs more than a root colour. Over a year the gap is small, because you come in about half as often.

Rough figures for short hair. In your first year the first-time blend adds to this, as do any maintenance toners.

So grey blending costs a little more over a year, for about half the appointments and no regrowth line in between. If you would rather keep every grey covered, full coverage is the cheaper visit. Our guide, grey blending vs full coverage, compares the two.

How to make your grey blend go further

  • Stretch the time between blends. There is no hard regrowth line, so you can go towards 12 weeks.
  • Top up the tone, not the whole blend. If only the tone has faded, a grey blend maintenance toner (from $220) refreshes it.
  • Look after it at home. A sulphate-free shampoo, heat protection and a purple shampoo once a week help the gloss last.
