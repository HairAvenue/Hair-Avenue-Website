Is Nanoplasty Good for Your Hair?

By the Hair Avenue Braddon team · Updated November 2026

For most hair, yes. Nanoplasty is a formaldehyde-free smoothing treatment that uses amino acids and natural proteins to repair and strengthen the hair from the inside. It suits most hair types, including colour-treated and damaged hair. The trade-offs are a long appointment and hair that dries much straighter than before.

What nanoplasty does to your hair

Unlike traditional keratin or chemical straightening, nanoplasty does not just coat the surface of the hair. Its amino acids and natural proteins work inside each strand, repairing and strengthening it from the inside out. Hair is left silky, shiny and far easier to manage.

What you get

  • Dramatically less frizz and volume.
  • Smoother, softer, shinier hair.
  • An easier, faster blow-dry at home.
  • Healthier-looking hair, with improved texture over time.
  • Results that last 6 to 12 months and grow out with no harsh line.

The trade-offs

  • It is a long appointment. Allow 3 to 4.5 hours.
  • Your hair will dry straighter. If you love your curls, nanoplasty is not the treatment for you.
  • It is sealed with heat. The treatment is finished with a smoothing iron. If your hair is very fragile or heavily bleached, book a 15-minute consultation ($35) first so we can check it is ready.
  • It is an investment. Nanoplasty is from $410, priced by hair length. See how much nanoplasty costs.

Is nanoplasty safe for coloured hair?

Yes. Nanoplasty is suitable for colour-treated hair, and many clients have both. If you are planning colour as well, tell us, and we will set the order and timing of the two services to protect your hair.

Who it suits best

  • Frizzy, wavy, curly or unruly hair that fights you every morning.
  • Thick hair that takes a long time to blow-dry.
  • Anyone who wants smooth hair for months without formaldehyde.

Who should choose something else

  • You want to keep your waves or curls. Hair SmoothFiller smooths frizz for 8–12 weeks and keeps your natural texture.
  • You want permanently straight hair. Shiseido Japanese straightening restructures the hair permanently. See perms and straightening.

Quick answers

Is nanoplasty good for your hair?

For most hair, yes. Nanoplasty is formaldehyde-free and uses amino acids and natural proteins to repair and strengthen the hair from the inside. It is suitable for most hair types, including colour-treated and damaged hair, and hair looks healthier with better texture over time.

Does nanoplasty damage your hair?

It is designed to do the opposite. The treatment repairs and strengthens the hair from the inside and is suitable for damaged hair. It is sealed with a smoothing iron, so if your hair is very fragile or heavily bleached, book a consultation first so we can check it is ready.

Is nanoplasty safe for coloured hair?

Yes. Nanoplasty is suitable for colour-treated hair. If you are having colour as well, we plan the order and timing of the two services to protect your hair.

Does nanoplasty contain formaldehyde?

No. Nanoplasty is formaldehyde-free. It uses amino acids and natural proteins instead.

Will nanoplasty take away my curls?

Nanoplasty makes hair dramatically smoother and straighter, with far less frizz and volume. If you want to keep your natural waves or curls and only lose the frizz, Hair SmoothFiller is the better choice.

How long do the results last?

Results last 6 to 12 months, depending on your hair type, texture and home care. A sulphate-free shampoo helps them last.
