Grey Blending vs Full Coverage: Which Is Right for You?

By Hair Avenue Braddon · Updated August 2026

Two very different philosophies

When silver starts appearing, you have two beautiful options — and they lead to quite different routines. Full coverage colours every strand for one rich, solid shade: polished, classic, and completely grey-free. Grey blending takes the opposite view — it works with your silver, weaving gentle highlights or lowlights through your hair and melting everything together with a demi-permanent gloss so the grey becomes a deliberate, dimensional feature rather than something to hide.

How each one lives on your head

The biggest practical difference is the regrowth line. With full coverage, new growth appears as a distinct line at your roots — most clients book a regrowth touch-up every 4–6 weeks to keep it invisible. With grey blending there is no hard line to chase: because your silver is blended rather than covered, the effect simply softens as it grows, and most clients refresh every 8–12 weeks.

Grey BlendingFull Coverage
LookSoft, dimensional, silver as a featureOne solid, uniform shade

Salon visitsRoughly every 8–12 weeksEvery 4–6 weeks
Best forFirst silvers, grow-outs, low maintenanceComplete coverage, classic single tone

Which should you choose?

Choose full coverage if you love a single, consistent shade and don’t mind a regular maintenance rhythm — modern colour like Wella Koleston Perfect ME+ delivers up to 100% grey coverage with far less damage than older formulas.

Choose grey blending if you want fewer salon visits, a softer grow-out, or you’re ready to transition towards natural silver without a jarring in-between stage. It’s also the graceful exit route if you’ve coloured for years and want out — the blend bridges old colour and new silver so every stage looks intentional.
