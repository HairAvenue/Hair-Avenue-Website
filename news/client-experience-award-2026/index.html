We’ve Won the 2026 Client Experience Award

Hair Avenue Braddon has been named a 2026 Phorest Client Experience Award winner — recognising a full year of outstanding client ratings, from real visits, in our Lonsdale Street salon.

Experience It Yourself
Home › News › 2026 Client Experience Award

Hair Avenue Braddon · 10 September 2026

Some awards are voted on by judges. This one was decided by the people who matter most — our clients.

The Client Experience Award is presented by Phorest, the booking platform behind tens of thousands of salons worldwide. It can’t be entered, bought or campaigned for: it goes only to salons whose clients consistently rate their visits as outstanding, measured across an entire year of real, verified appointments.

What the award recognises

  • An average client rating above 4 out of 5 stars — sustained across a full year (March 2025 to February 2026)
  • A minimum of 60 verified client reviews, each tied to a real appointment
  • Ratings collected independently by Phorest after each visit — not hand-picked testimonials

For our team, it’s the most meaningful kind of recognition there is. Every rating behind this award came from someone who sat in our chairs — for a colour, a cut, a blow wave, a first-ever grey blend — and took a moment afterwards to say it was worth it.

“To every client who trusted us this year — thank you. This one’s yours. We opened Hair Avenue believing that if you treat every person in the chair like the only person in the room, everything else follows. This award says our clients feel it too.”— Kelly, Founder & Owner, Hair Avenue Braddon

What it means when you book with us

Nothing changes — and that’s the point. The same care that earned this award is waiting for every client, every visit: premium Wella Professionals products only, honest consultations, and a team that genuinely loves what they do. If you haven’t visited yet, there’s never been a better reason.

You can read what our clients say on our reviews page — 4.9 stars across 180+ Google reviews and counting.
