Great Lengths Tape-In Hair Extensions Have Arrived in Braddon

Hair Avenue is now an official Great Lengths salon. Longer, fuller hair in a single appointment, colour-matched to your own, with every stylist on our team trained to fit it.

Home › News › Great Lengths Tape Extensions

Hair Avenue Braddon · 23 September 2026

For years, the one thing we couldn’t offer at Hair Avenue was length. If your hair wouldn’t grow past your shoulders, or a big cut had left you missing the swish, we could colour it and cut it beautifully — but we couldn’t give you more of it. From this month, we can.

Hair Avenue Braddon is now an official Great Lengths salon, fitting Great Lengths Essenza tape-in extensions in Canberra. Great Lengths is the extension brand trusted by salons around the world, and we chose their tapes for the same reason we only use Wella colour: if it isn’t the best for your hair, it doesn’t come through our door.

Why we chose Great Lengths tapes

  • About 20% smaller and lighter than standard tapes, so they hide easily — even in finer hair
  • Medical-grade, hypoallergenic adhesive that sits flat and comfortably against the scalp
  • Reusable: moved up every 6–8 weeks, with the same hair re-taped up to three times
  • Clean, residue-free removal with a specialised remover — no pulling, no damage

How tape-in extensions work

A small section of your own hair is sandwiched between two slim wefts of real hair, each with a fine adhesive strip, so the tapes lie completely flat. A full head takes about an hour to fit for a small set, and the result moves like your own hair because it is real hair, colour-matched at the root, mid-lengths and ends.

As your hair grows, the tapes move with it. Every 6 to 8 weeks we take them out, re-tape them and move them back up to the root, then wash and blow wave so you leave finished. The same Great Lengths hair is reused each time, which makes tapes the lower-commitment, better-value way into extensions.

GL Tapes in place — flat, even and hidden under your own hair

“We waited until we found an extension system we would put in our own hair. Great Lengths tapes are light, they’re gentle on the hair underneath, and they come out clean. Now every stylist on our team is trained to fit them, so you can book with whoever already does your colour.”— Kelly, Founder & Owner, Hair Avenue Braddon

What it costs

It starts with a 15-minute consultation and colour match ($35). We check your hair’s condition and density, work out how many pieces you need and match Great Lengths Essenza hair against your own in daylight. You leave with an exact price, not a guess off a website.

From there, application is from $270 for up to 10 pieces, move-ups every 6–8 weeks are from $280 with removal and a blow wave included, and removal on its own is from $100. Bringing your own hair, or wearing extensions from another salon? We fit, move up and remove those too. Full pricing, the piece tiers and all the answers are on our hair extensions page.

Frequently asked questions

What are Great Lengths tape-in extensions?

Slim wefts of real hair with a medical-grade adhesive strip, sandwiched around a small section of your own hair so they lie flat against the scalp. Great Lengths Essenza tapes are about 20% smaller than standard tapes, are moved up every 6–8 weeks, and the same hair can be reused up to three times.

How much do tape-in hair extensions cost at Hair Avenue?

The consultation and colour match is $35. Application is from $270 for up to 10 pieces ($170 plus $10 per piece), move-ups every 6–8 weeks are from $280 and removal is from $100. The Great Lengths hair itself is quoted at your consultation, so you leave with an exact price.

How do I book Great Lengths extensions in Canberra?

Book a 15-minute Consultation & Colour Match ($35) online, call 0449 801 937, or visit us at Unit 102/16 Lonsdale Street, Braddon. Every stylist on the team is Great Lengths–trained.
