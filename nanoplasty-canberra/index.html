What is nanoplasty?

Nanoplasty is a premium Brazilian hair smoothing treatment. Unlike traditional keratin or chemical straightening, it is formaldehyde-free and uses amino acids and natural proteins to repair and strengthen your hair from the inside out. It does not just coat the surface: it rebuilds each strand.

The result is dramatically smoother, straighter, glossier hair, with far less frizz and volume, that takes a fraction of the time to blow-dry at home.

Who it suits

Nanoplasty is ideal for frizzy, wavy, curly or unruly hair, and for anyone who spends too long each morning with a blow dryer or irons. It is suitable for most hair types, including colour-treated and damaged hair.

What to expect at your nanoplasty appointment

  • A look at your hair — we check its length, thickness and condition, and talk through the result you want.
  • The nanoplasty treatment — applied through your hair, section by section.
  • Blow dry and heat seal — your hair is dried, then the treatment is sealed in with the smoothing iron.
  • The reveal — a blow wave or iron finish, so you leave with it styled.

Allow 3 to 4.5 hours, depending on your hair length and thickness. Bring a book or your laptop, and we’ll look after the coffee. You can add a haircut in the same visit.

Nanoplasty: frequently asked questions

What is nanoplasty?

Nanoplasty is a premium Brazilian hair smoothing treatment. It is formaldehyde-free and uses amino acids and natural proteins to repair and strengthen the hair from the inside out, leaving it silky, shiny and far less frizzy. It does not just coat the surface: it rebuilds each strand.

How much does nanoplasty cost in Canberra?

At Hair Avenue Braddon nanoplasty is $410 for short hair, $549 for medium, $599 for long and $690 for extra long or very thick hair. On weekdays, medium is $520, long $570 and extra long $650. The price includes the treatment, the smoothing iron process and a blow wave or iron finish. If your hair needs extra product on the day, an additional $50 to $100 may apply.

How long does nanoplasty last?

Results last 6 to 12 months, depending on your hair type, texture and home care. A sulphate-free shampoo helps them last. Because nanoplasty works inside the hair rather than coating it, it grows out with no harsh line.

How long does a nanoplasty appointment take?

Allow 3 to 4.5 hours, depending on your hair length and thickness.

Is nanoplasty good for your hair?

For most hair, yes. It is formaldehyde-free, and the amino acids and natural proteins repair and strengthen the hair from the inside. It suits most hair types, including colour-treated and damaged hair. Our guide, is nanoplasty good for your hair?, covers the trade-offs.

Is nanoplasty better than keratin?

Nanoplasty is formaldehyde-free and works by rebuilding the hair fibre with amino acids rather than coating it, so the result looks more natural, lasts longer and is gentler on your hair and scalp. Our guide, nanoplasty vs keratin, compares the two.

Will nanoplasty make my hair straight?

It makes hair dramatically smoother and straighter, with far less frizz and volume, and much quicker to blow-dry. If you love your waves and only want less frizz, SmoothFiller keeps your natural texture. If you want permanently poker-straight hair, ask about Shiseido straightening.

Can I colour my hair as well?

Yes. Nanoplasty is suitable for colour-treated hair, and our stylists regularly pair it with colour. We plan the order and timing of the two services with you to protect your hair.

What is the aftercare?

It is simple. There is no strict no-wash window, and a sulphate-free shampoo helps your results last. The smoothness grows out gradually rather than washing out.

Is nanoplasty cheaper on weekdays?

Yes, for medium and longer hair. On weekdays, medium is $520, long $570 and extra long or very thick $650, and you can add a haircut for $45 (normally $65). Short hair is $410 on any day.

Do I need a consultation before nanoplasty?

No, you can book straight in online by hair length. If you are not sure it suits your hair, or your hair is very fragile, book a 15-minute consultation ($35) first. Consultations are available Monday to Friday.

Where can I get nanoplasty in Canberra?

Hair Avenue Braddon offers nanoplasty at Unit 102/16 Lonsdale Street, Braddon, a few minutes from Civic, from $410. The salon is rated 4.9 stars from 200+ Google reviews, and parking and the light rail are close by.
